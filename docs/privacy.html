Privacy Policy

Live Transcript for Microsoft Teams (browser extension) · Effective 20 November 2026

In short: the extension reads the meeting transcript that is already shown on your Teams page, keeps a copy in your own browser, and saves it as a text file on your computer when you ask it to. It never sends anything anywhere. There is no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking.

What the extension reads

Only on teams.microsoft.com and teams.cloud.microsoft, and only what Microsoft Teams already displays to you:

  • the text, speaker names and times in the meeting's Transcript pane, or the live captions, while they are open;
  • the meeting's name and the page title, to name the saved file.

It does not read your chats, files, contacts, email, calendar, audio or video, and it does not run on any other website.

Where the data is kept

  • In your browser. While a meeting is being recorded, a backup of its transcript is kept in the extension's local storage on your computer, so it isn't lost if the tab closes. The 15 most recent meetings are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • On your computer. When you click Save, a .txt file is written to your Downloads folder (Downloads/Teams Transcripts/).

Nothing is uploaded, synced, sold or shared with anyone, including the developer.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single meeting's backup with its Delete button in the extension's popup.
  • Removing the extension from your browser deletes all of its backups.
  • Saved .txt files are ordinary files on your computer; delete them like any other file.

Other people in your meetings

A transcript contains what other participants said. Please follow your organisation's or school's rules about keeping meeting records, and let participants know if you keep a copy.
